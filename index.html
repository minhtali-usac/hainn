Current Interests

Probing the Universe's
Darkest Objects

The Black Hole Mass Spectrum

Simulating and measuring the masses of central black holes across the full spectrum of galaxy hosts, from intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs; 103–106 M☉) to supermassive black holes (SMBHs; >106 M☉), using high-resolution spectroscopy.

Galaxy Formation & Evolution

Galaxy formation remains a central problem of modern cosmology. Comparing galaxies over a wide range of distances traces their assembly histories, from nearby systems with well-resolved evolutionary records to distant ones that probe the earliest epochs of the Universe.

Nuclear Star Cluster Dynamics

Simulating ELT observations with HARMONI's ultra-high-resolution integral-field spectroscopy in order to characterise the morphology, stellar content, kinematics, and dynamical masses of nuclear star clusters in low-mass early-type galaxies.

ALMA Molecular Gas Dynamics

Modelling molecular gas observed at millimetre wavelengths with ALMA to measure supermassive black hole masses in massive elliptical and spiral galaxies, and thereby to constrain their growth histories over cosmic time.
